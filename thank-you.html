Three Steps. In This Order.

Most people download something and set it aside. Don't do that. Here's the only sequence that matters:

1

Complete the Paycheck Drain Audit — today or tomorrow

Open your Tools Workbook (download it above) and go to the Paycheck Drain Audit tab — the first tab in the workbook. This takes 15 minutes. You'll answer a few specific questions about where your money goes and finish knowing your single biggest cash leak — with a number attached to it. Do this before your next payday if you can.

You don't need to fix anything yet. Just find the leak. Clarity comes before the plan.

2

Build your routing plan with the Paycheck-First Calculator

Once you've done the audit, move to the Paycheck-First Calculator tab in the same workbook. This is the one-time setup — budget about an hour, ideally before your next payday. You'll enter your actual obligations and build a routing plan that tells your money where to go the moment it arrives, before anything else has a chance to interrupt it. Set it up once, and the system runs itself.

If something unexpected comes up mid-cycle before you've set it up — open The Paycheck Rescue Plan bonus first. It's built for exactly that situation.

3

Start the 30/60/90 Day Cushion Tracker on your next payday

After your routing plan is set up, open the Tracker tab on your next payday. You're watching one number: your cushion balance. Not every transaction. Not a detailed budget. Just that one number, moving in the right direction, one payday at a time. The 5-Minute Weekly Check Card tab keeps you on track between paydays — three questions, five minutes.
